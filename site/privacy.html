Privacy Policy

Grazia Stones app (Android and iPhone) · Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Grazia Stones app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. It is written to comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email address, phone number (if you add it), company name, and your sign-in method (email and password, or Google).
  • Enquiries: details you type into quote and sample requests: contact details, city or delivery address, area and the designs you chose.
  • AI Room Studio: room photos you upload and the images generated from them.
  • AI usage: how many AI images and design-assistant (Aarna) messages your account used, and the amount of AI processing (tokens) each used, to apply plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • Subscriptions: if you buy Grazia Plus or Pro, the plan, its status and expiry, and the purchase token from Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Saved items: wishlist, cart, saved designs and saved addresses.
  • Approximate city: only when you tap "Detect location" on a quote, sample or address form, the app looks up your approximate city from your IP address using the ipwho.is service, to fill in the form. The app does not use GPS.
  • Device and diagnostics: device model, operating system and app version, and crash reports.

2. Camera and Live AR

  • The camera is used only while you use Live AR or take a room photo for AI Room Studio.
  • Live AR runs on your phone: wall detection, depth and measurements are computed on the device. The camera feed is not recorded or uploaded.
  • A room photo leaves your phone only when you choose to generate an AI image with it.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To answer quotes and sample requests and contact you about them.
  • To generate AI room visualisations and design suggestions you ask for.
  • To apply AI usage limits and manage subscriptions.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, Singapore region), on our behalf.
  • Google Gemini API: room photos and design-assistant messages are sent to Google to generate the image or answer.
  • Vercel: hosts the app's server functions that call the AI service.
  • ipwho.is: receives your IP address when you tap "Detect location".
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store, for subscription purchases.
  • The Grazia Stones team, to handle your quotes, samples and support.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS). The database uses row-level security so each account can read only its own records; staff access is limited to administrators.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or ask us to delete it, or only some of your data, as described on our account and data deletion page. Order records, if any, may be kept only as long as required for accounting and tax law.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to us. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults planning a home or project. It is not directed at children under 18.
