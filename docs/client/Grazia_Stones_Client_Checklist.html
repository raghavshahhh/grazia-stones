What we need from you to finish the launch

Everything below is a one time setup. Each step lists who does it and roughly how long it takes. Once these are done, RAGSPRO completes the rest.

Step 1. Verify the Grazia Gmail account You, 10 minutes

The address contactgraziastones@gmail.com is now the contact email inside the app, on Google Play and the admin login. It must be fully secured.

  1. Open myaccount.google.com and sign in with contactgraziastones@gmail.com.
  2. Go to Security, then 2-Step Verification, and turn it on (use the owner's phone number).
  3. Under Security, add a Recovery email and a Recovery phone that belong to the business owner, not to an employee.
  4. Open Gmail and confirm you can receive mail. Send us a screenshot of the inbox so we know it works.
  5. Tell RAGSPRO the full name of the person who owns this account.

Step 2. Add the business phone number You, 5 minutes

  1. Choose one mobile number that is always with the owner. Google, Apple and Razorpay send verification codes to it.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com, then Personal info, then Phone, and add the number.
  3. Send the same number to RAGSPRO. We add it to the Google Play Console contact details and to the support page of the app.
  4. Also confirm the number customers should call and WhatsApp (it is shown on the Contact and Dealers screens).

Step 3. Turn on billing for the Gemini AI key You, 20 minutes

The AI Room Studio and the Aarna assistant use Google Gemini. Gemini needs a billing account so that image generation keeps working during busy hours. The key is stored on our server only. It is never placed inside the app.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com and sign in with contactgraziastones@gmail.com.
  2. Click Get API key, then Create API key. Choose Create API key in new project and name it grazia-stones-prod.
  3. Open console.cloud.google.com/billing with the same Gmail and click Create account (or Link a billing account).
  4. Choose country India, add a business debit or credit card. International and recurring payments must be enabled on the card.
  5. Link this billing account to the project grazia-stones-prod.
  6. In Budgets and alerts create a monthly budget (for example Rs 2,000) with email alerts at 50%, 90% and 100%. This protects you from surprise charges.
  7. Share the API key with RAGSPRO using a private channel (a call, or a password manager link). Please do not send it in a public chat or as a screenshot. If it is ever exposed, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one.
Gemini charges only for what is used. RAGSPRO will add a daily limit per user inside the app so costs stay predictable.

Sign-up emails. New users get a confirmation email. The free default sender allows very few emails per hour, so some customers would not receive it. To fix this we need a sender address on your own domain:

Step 5. Apple App Store (iPhone app) Needed for iPhone

An iPhone version needs an Apple Developer account in the name of the business. The annual fee is paid by the account owner (USD 99).

  1. Confirm the exact legal business name that must appear as the seller on the App Store (for example "Grazia Stones Private Limited"). Apple shows this name publicly.
  2. If it is a company, share its D-U-N-S number. If you do not have one, it is free and takes about 1 to 2 weeks at developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup.
  3. Choose who will sign in as the account holder. That person will receive Apple's verification call.
  4. Share the company website, and a working business email and phone number.
