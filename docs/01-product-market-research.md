## 10. Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Dealer Resistance
**Mitigation**:
- Free tier with immediate value
- Success-based pricing model
- Dedicated support team
- Show competitor adoption (FOMO)

### Risk 2: Technology Adoption Hesitation
**Mitigation**:
- Simple onboarding (3 minutes to first AR experience)
- Video tutorials & tooltips
- In-app support chat
- Dealer demo events

### Risk 3: AR Accuracy Issues
**Mitigation**:
- Beta testing with real customers
- Disclaimer about visualization accuracy
- Encourage physical sample confirmation
- Continuous AI model improvement

### Risk 4: Competition Response
**Mitigation**:
- Speed to market (first-mover advantage)
- Lock-in architect partnerships
- White-label diversification
- Patent AI/AR algorithms

### Risk 5: Content Management Overhead
**Mitigation**:
- Automated product ingestion (CSV/API)
- Bulk upload tools
- Dealer-submitted photography
- Photography vendor partnerships
