## Full description (≈2,150/4,000)
Grazia Stones brings a complete natural-stone and cultured-stone catalogue to your phone. Browse wall cladding designs, see how they look, calculate what your project needs, and send a quote enquiry to our team in a few taps.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Browse the catalogue — explore 36 curated collections and 90+ designs of stone cladding, 3D panels and feature-wall finishes, with size, thickness, finish and coverage details for each.

• Visualize on your wall — the Wall & Tile Visualizer shows a stone on a wall of your size in 3D, and estimates net area, wastage and the number of boxes you need.

• Try it in your space — Live AR places designs on your wall using your camera on supported ARCore devices. Take a virtual walk through our VR showroom to see designs in living rooms, hotel lobbies and more.

• AI Room Studio — upload a photo of your room and a stone design to preview how the wall could look.

• Request a quote — pick one or several stones, add your project area and city, and send an enquiry. Our team confirms final pricing and availability with you directly.

• Request physical samples — order sample swatches so you can see and feel the stone before you decide.

• Save your favourites — keep designs in your wishlist and build a project list of stones to quote together.

• Find us — locate Grazia Stones showrooms and authorized dealers and get directions or call the desk.

GOOD TO KNOW

• Prices in the app are indicative. The final price, availability and delivery are confirmed in your quote.
• The app does not take payments — quotes and payments are arranged directly with Grazia Stones.
• Browse as a guest, or sign in with your phone number, email or Google to save your wishlist, designs and requests.
• You can delete your account and data any time from Profile → Delete Account.
